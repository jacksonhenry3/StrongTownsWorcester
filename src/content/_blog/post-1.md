## Upcoming Events & Meetings

Our next working meeting is **Sunday, September 7th, from 2pm-5pm** at Technocopia (44 Portland Street). We will be preparing for **Park(ing) Day**, where we will temporarily transform two parking spaces into a vibrant public parklet from September 19th–21st.

## Local Elections

* **Primary Election:** Tuesday, September 2nd.
* **General Election:** November 4th (Register by Oct 9th).
The Worcester Urban Planning Partnership has released a candidate questionnaire to highlight housing, land use, and mobility issues.

## Updates & Advocacy

We are actively seeking volunteers for financial management and newsletter writing. Our ongoing advocacy continues for the **Now|Next Plan**, **Vision Zero**, and the **Mobility Action Plan**. We have also completed the **Municipal Finance Decoder** for Worcester to better understand our city’s fiscal health.

## Get Involved

Join the conversation on our Discord, Instagram, or Facebook. If you’d like to volunteer for Park(ing) Day or outreach, email us at **strongtowns.worcester@gmail.com**.
