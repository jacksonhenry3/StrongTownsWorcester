Hello Worcester Strong Towners! Our next general meeting is **Saturday, August 8th, from 4 PM to 6 PM**. This meeting, we are planning to do a Worcester litter clean-up with [Ipickupworcesterlitterverywell](https://www.instagram.com/ipickupworcesterlitterverywell/) and [Ella from Sustainable Worcester](https://sites.google.com/view/sustainableworcester/home?authuser=0). We’re planning on walking the same route as we did for our walk audit, which ran from Worcester Common and down a portion of Southbridge Street. **We’ll meet at the intersection of Southbridge and Southgate Streets**.

## Action Items

**Out to Lunch:** If you’re in the downtown area on **July 29th, August 12th, or August 19th from 10 AM to 3 PM**, come down to the Worcester Common! Worcester Strong Towns will have a table at “Out to Lunch,” a weekly event during the summer with a variety of vendors, live music, and information booths. Come support us and all the local businesses and community organizations that make our city tick!

**Worcester Bike Party:** On the last Friday of July and August (July 31st and August 28th), Worcester Earn-a-Bike is hosting the [Worcester Bike Party](https://www.facebook.com/worcesterbikeparty/)! Meet at the Worcester Common at 8 PM. The June event was a smash hit! All riders and bikes welcome.

**Sustainable Worcester:** Sustainable Worcester shares resources on how to live sustainably in our city. That includes a calendar of events! You can check out [their website here](https://sites.google.com/view/sustainableworcester/home?authuser=0), and [their calendar at this link](https://sites.google.com/view/sustainableworcester/calendar?authuser=0)!

**Regional Pedestrian Plan:** The Central Massachusetts Regional Planning Commission (CMRPC) is beginning work on updating its Regional Pedestrian Plan. [Take its survey](https://survey123.arcgis.com/share/2ce9da39e8254376ac4d676452754315) to make your voice heard on your walking experience in Central Massachusetts!

**City Council:** Worcester’s City Council is not meeting until **August 18th**. You can view the upcoming agendas and all of its other files [here](https://worcesterma.primegov.com/public/portal) (just look for City Council under “Meeting Title” on the left).

**WRTA Bus Tracker:** If you didn’t know, the Worcester Regional Transit Authority (WRTA) released an updated bus tracker online. [Use it here](https://swiv.wrta.cadavl.com/SWIV/WRTA).

**Call to Action!** We’re putting out feelers for any Strong Towns members who would be open to going to City boards and commissions meetings – especially those most related to Strong Towns interests (such as the Planning Board or the Zoning Board of Appeals). Let’s get that conversation started on our Discord!

## Ongoing Efforts

- We continue to advocate for the [Now|Next Plan](https://www.worcesterma.gov/worcester-now-next), the [Vision Zero Safety Action Plan](https://www.worcesterma.gov/mobility/vision-zero), the [Mobility Action Plan](https://www.worcesterma.gov/mobility/worcester-mobility-action-plan), and the [Housing Production Plan](https://www.worcesterma.gov/housing-neighborhood-development/document-center/worcester-housing-production-plan-final.pdf).

## Social Media

We have an Instagram, a Discord, a Facebook group, a Facebook page, and a Bluesky. We’d love to see you there and hear your thoughts! If you have any pictures or other Worcester-specific content you’d like us to post, please share!

Thank you for your continued interest! We look forward to bringing you more in the future.
