# November Update: Elections, Transit, and Housing

Hello Worcester Strong Towners!

**Next Meeting:** Our next meeting is this **Saturday, December 13th, from 4pm-5:30pm** at **Technocopia at 44 Portland Street** (please use the furthest door to the left and the intercom). Our focus for this meeting will be working through and beginning to plan our priority campaigns for 2026.

## Action Items

**City Council Elections:** City council elections are this fall! The **general election is November 4th**, so if you have not voted yet, you still have time!

Here is a website you can use to find your polling location; polls will be open from 7am to 8pm. If you don’t know who is running, here is a resource to help get you up to speed.

**Candidate Questionnaire:** The **Worcester Urban Planning Partnership**, of which Strong Towns Worcester is a member, has released a city council candidate questionnaire for the November election. We are getting the word out that **housing, land use planning, transportation, and mobility are on the ballot this year!** If you’re interested in how candidates responded, please take a look.

**WRTA Survey:** The **Worcester Regional Transit Authority (WRTA)** currently has a survey open for a comprehensive service analysis project which will help shape bus service in Worcester and surrounding communities in the upcoming years. You can take the survey here!

**Vernon Connected:** This is a planning effort regarding the future of the Vernon Street Bridge and the surrounding neighborhoods. These are neighborhoods that were separated by I-290 decades ago, and this is an opportunity to reconnect them.

**Transit Meeting:** The **Transit for Worcester Coalition** will be hosting a community meeting on **December 3 at 6 PM** regarding establishing a bus rapid transit system in Worcester. Bus rapid transit enables faster busing through dedicated bus lanes and other infrastructure improvements. The meeting will be held at the **Worcester Main Library, 3 Salem Street**, and you can get more information and RSVP here: [https://www.linkedin.com/events/movingworcesterforward7389095389276172288/](https://www.linkedin.com/events/movingworcesterforward7389095389276172288/)

**Leadership Roles:** If you are interested in a Strong Towns Worcester leadership role of any kind over the next year, please contact strongtowns.worcester@gmail.com. This can be for the whole year or to be on call for events and initiatives.

## Updates

The **Strong Towns national organization** has begun to release toolkits for a **“Housing-Ready City.”** These include “Six Policies that will Make Your Town Housing Ready” and “How Cities Can Grow an Ecosystem of Incremental Developers.” [Take a look here!](https://www.strongtowns.org/housingready "null")

## Ongoing Efforts

- We continue to advocate for the **Now|Next Plan**, the **Vision Zero Safety Action Plan**, the **Mobility Action Plan**, and the **Housing Production Plan**.
