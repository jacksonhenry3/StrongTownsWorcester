Hello, Worcester Strong Towners! Our next **general meeting is Saturday, September 12th, at 4 PM**. At this meeting, we will perform a walk audit down Grove Street to Lincoln Square and back (1.3 mi). **We’ll meet at Fuel America Café (100 Grove St) at 4 PM.**

**Thank you** to everyone that attended our August meeting. We picked up litter along Southbridge Street with Sustainable Worcester and [Ipickupworcesterlitterverywell](https://www.instagram.com/ipickupworcesterlitterverywell/). All told, our volunteers collected **twenty-eight** bags of trash, equal to **290.5 lbs!!**

## Action Items

**Question 7:** November’s ballot Question 7 is **Legalize Starter Homes**! This is an initiative to update zoning laws across Massachusetts to allow single-family homes on smaller lots than may be the case in most communities. [See this Ballotpedia page for a quick rundown on what a “YES” and a “NO” vote would mean](https://ballotpedia.org/Massachusetts_Question_7,_Limit_on_Required_Lot_Size_for_Single-Family_Homes_Initiative_(2026)).

**Door Knocking:** To participate in door knocking for this question, please RSVP for [October 3rd at this link](https://action.abundanthousingma.org/a/legalize-starter-homes-knock-across-worcester-103), and / or [October 17th at this one](https://action.abundanthousingma.org/a/lsh-knock-across-worcester-1017)!

**Learn More:** [Check out the official Strong Towns podcast with an interview](https://www.strongtowns.org/podcasts/2026-02-10-andrew-mikula-bottom-up-shorts) with Andrew Mikula from the Legalize Starter Homes initiative.

**Community Bike Ride:** Join us on **September 19th at 10 AM** for a community bike ride on the Worcester portions of the [Blackstone River Multi-Use Trail](https://blackstoneheritagecorridor.org/exploring-the-blackstone-river-valley/maps-tours-guides/blackstone-river-bikeway/). Stay connected to our Instagram and Discord for more information as the event gets closer!

**Sustainable Worcester:** Sustainable Worcester shares resources on how to live sustainably in our city. That includes a calendar of events! You can check [out their website here](https://sites.google.com/view/sustainableworcester/home?authuser=0), and [their calendar at this link](https://sites.google.com/view/sustainableworcester/calendar?authuser=0)!

**City Council:** Worcester’s City Council’s next meeting is **September 8th**. [You can view the upcoming agendas and all of its other files here](https://worcesterma.primegov.com/public/portal) (just look for City Council under “Meeting Title” on the left).

**Call to Action!** We will be compiling the information we’ve received from walk audits into a tangible report. If you’re **interested in helping to write walk audit reports**, please contact Will Talbot on Discord or at wmtz15@gmail.com!
