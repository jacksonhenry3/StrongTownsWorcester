## Action Items

**Earth Day at WPI:** We will be tabling an Earth Day event at WPI on **April 22nd, from 4:30 PM to 7 PM** at the WPI Quadrangle at 100 Institute Road.

**City Council:** Worcester’s City Council is meeting on **March 31st at 6:30 PM** in the Esther Howland Chamber (on the third floor of City Hall). You can view the upcoming agendas and all of its other files [here](https://worcesterma.primegov.com/public/portal) (just look for City Council under “Meeting Title” on the left). You can watch in person or online.

- This week, there are multiple petitions for crosswalk repainting and crosswalk traffic control timing reviews on Shrewsbury St.
- There are 30 councilors’ orders on the agenda. Among them, there is a request for a report on how the City handles 311 requests (10a), whether a left turn is allowed on Norton Drive onto Shore Drive (10d), whether the city can offer to sell 50-gallon buckets for recycling to residents (10e), and a request for a list of current Payment in Lieu of Taxes agreements (10f).
- Interestingly, 10i requests a draft ordinance to impose “escalating fees onto underdeveloped lots that have been underdeveloped and vacant for more than five years.”
- 11b requests the City Manager organize a volunteer cleanup day for Burncoat St at Mountain St. East – apparently long an illegal dumping area.

**Planning Board:** The Planning Board is meeting on **April 1, at 5:30 PM**, in the Levi Lincoln Chamber (on the third floor of City Hall), and you can also [join via Zoom](https://worcesterma.zoomgov.com/j/1617314979). You can view [all application materials here](https://www.worcesterma.gov/planning-regulatory/boards/planning-board). They will be discussing: 0 Chester Street, 28 Harrington Way, 1103 Millbury Street, 3 Dundee Court, 12 Malden Street, 17 Pearl Street, 540 West Boylston Street, 0 and 2 Tyson Road and 0 Burncoat Street, 25 West Chester Street, and portions of the Belmont Street/Plantation Street Corridor/Green Hill Area.

**Vernon Connected:** This is a planning effort regarding the future of the Vernon Street Bridge and the surrounding neighborhoods. These are neighborhoods that were separated by I-290 decades ago, and this is an opportunity to reconnect them.

- You can learn more about Vernon Connected [here](https://vernon-connected-cmrpc.hub.arcgis.com/). There is also a Vernon Connected survey, located at that link.
- If you’ve ever encountered an issue with the transportation network in this area, you can add a marker to [this interactive map](https://engagestantec.com/vernon-connected) to bring the project team’s attention to it.

**Vision Zero:** The Central Massachusetts Regional Planning Commission (CMRPC) is developing a Regional Vision Zero Action Plan (Regional Safe Streets for All), covering all of central and southern Worcester County. CMRPC is looking for feedback in the development of the plan.

- There is a [short survey here](https://survey123.arcgis.com/share/a3f878b9dac441b3a5468927f5962472) to help CMRPC identify and prioritize projects across the region meant to eliminate fatalities on the region’s roadways.
- There is also a [concern mapper (here)](https://cmrpc.maps.arcgis.com/apps/instant/reporter/index.html?appid=5d6761593db6441a8c0b5c55962be22d) where you can specifically identify areas on the map where you have mobility concerns (like a dangerous intersection, or areas where biking is dodgy, for example).

**Neighborhood Connect:** Neighborhood Connect is a free training program to learn strategies for making change in your neighborhood. It is a research study led by the Prevention Research Center (PRC) at UMass Chan in partnership with the Coalition for a Healthy Greater Worcester, Worcester Division of Public Health, and WalkMassachusetts. They are currently taking sign-ups from people associated with Main South/Piedmont Street, and Quinsigamond Village/Whipple Apartments/Route 20.

- If you live, work, or volunteer in a Worcester neighborhood, you can join as part of a team or on your own. You’ll meet with others 6 times on Zoom, and once in person, to learn what has worked in other communities and how to work in your own!
- There are also 3 surveys to complete over one year – and receive a $20 gift card for each one!
- [You can learn more by clicking here](https://arcsapps.umassmed.edu/redcap/surveys/?s=P99889CMYDMFXWCC) or by emailing the program directly.

**WRTA Bus Tracker:** If you didn’t know, the Worcester Regional Transit Authority (WRTA) released an updated bus tracker online. [Use it here](https://swiv.wrta.cadavl.com/SWIV/WRTA).

**Call to Action!** In February, Worcester’s Department of Transportation and Mobility (DTM) held a public session to [present a redesign of the Newton Square roundabout and the half-mile of Highland Street](https://www.worcesterma.gov/mobility/projects-engineering/newton-square-highland-street-improvement-project) in front of Doherty High School. [There are currently two proposed redesigns of Highland Street](https://www.worcesterma.gov/mobility/document-center/highland-street-alternatives.pdf). We highly recommend calling (508-929-1300, ext 49500) or emailing DTM and politely advocating for Alternative 1: the Separated Bike Lane. We still have a chance for our voices to be heard in the continued construction of a Worcester bike network!

We’re putting out feelers for any Strong Towns members who would be open to going to City boards and commissions meetings – especially those most related to Strong Towns interests (such as the Planning Board or the Zoning Board of Appeals). Let’s get that conversation started on our Discord!
